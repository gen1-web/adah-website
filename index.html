A morning that protects your focus before the world takes it

Imagine waking up, praying Fajr with full presence, and knowing exactly what you'll build today. This is how to get there.

  1. Wake for Fajr, not for your feed

    Charge your phone outside the bedroom. The first thing your mind meets sets the tone for the day.

  2. Pray with khushu'

    Slow down your first two rak'ahs. Presence is a skill, and this is where you train it.

  3. Morning adhkar and a few ayat

    Five quiet minutes of remembrance before any input. Let your heart settle before the world gets a say.

  4. Set one niyyah for the day

    Write one meaningful task on paper. One intention, clearly made, beats a long list you never start.

  5. One deep block before Dhuhr

    Ninety minutes, phone in another room. Then let Dhuhr be your recalibration point.
